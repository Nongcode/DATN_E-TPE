# Quy trinh chuan hoa gia san pham ETEK

## Muc tieu

Gia san pham trong he thong duoc chuan hoa de phuc vu demo va phan bien, tranh tinh trang nhap gia tuy y hoac sai lech bac so 0. Gia hien tai trong bang `Product.price` duoc xem la gia ban de xuat. Gia gach ngang tren giao dien storefront duoc he thong tu tinh cao hon 15% bang filter `markup_price` de mo phong nguyen gia/gia niem yet.

## Nguyen tac dinh gia

1. Phan nhom san pham truoc khi nhap gia: thiet bi lop/nang ha, rua xe/detailing, hoa chat, dung cu garage, camera/phu kien.
2. Voi thiet bi may moc, gia tang theo cong suat, muc tu dong hoa, phu kien di kem, thuong hieu va xuat xu.
3. Voi hoa chat va vat tu tieu hao, gia bam theo dung tich/quy cach dong goi; chai/can nho khong duoc de gia o muc hang chuc trieu.
4. Voi san pham nhap khau hoac thuong hieu chuyen nghiep nhu CORGHI/GYS/Ma-Fra, gia duoc dat o phan khuc cao hon san pham pho thong.
5. Khong de san pham kinh doanh co gia 0d. Neu chua co bao gia that, dung muc gia demo hop ly va ghi chu la gia tham khao.

## Cac loi du lieu da xu ly

- Sua cac san pham co gia 0d: tu dung cu 8 ngan, may ra vao lop A224.
- Sua cac hoa chat bi lech bac so 0: glass cleaner 500ml, Ma-Fra Killer, CROMOBRILL 2G 6kg.
- Nang gia nhom may rua xe 3 pha theo cong suat/ap luc thay vi de ngang voi may gia dinh.
- Nang gia nhom CORGHI/GYS theo phan khuc thiet bi garage chuyen nghiep.

## Nguon tham khao va cach giai thich

- Ma-Fra la nhom san pham cham soc/rua xe chuyen nghiep, co cac dong car wash va detailing rieng: https://mafra.it/en/
- May rua ap luc cao dan dung 160-180 bar da co RRP khoang vai tram bang Anh o thi truong quoc te; may garage 3 pha cong suat 4-5.5kW can dat cao hon dong dan dung: https://www.idealhome.co.uk/house-manual/cleaning/best-pressure-washers
- May ra vao lop la thiet bi chuyen dung co ban kep, he khi nen, co cau ep/tach mep lop va cac phien ban tu dong/ban tu dong; vi vay khong the dinh gia nhu dung cu cam tay: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tire_changer

## Cau tra loi ngan khi hoi dong hoi

Em khong nhap gia tuy y. Em chuan hoa gia theo nhom san pham, thong so ky thuat va phan khuc thuong hieu. Voi san pham tieu hao nhu hoa chat, gia phu thuoc dung tich/quy cach; voi thiet bi garage, gia phu thuoc cong suat, muc tu dong hoa va phu kien. Nhung gia bat thuong nhu 0d hoac lech bac so 0 da duoc ra soat va chinh lai. Trong demo, day la gia ban de xuat; con gia niem yet/gach ngang tren giao dien duoc he thong tu tinh cao hon 15% de the hien khuyen mai.
